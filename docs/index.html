Why “Lensatic”

A lensatic compass is the Army’s field compass. You sight a landmark through its lens, read the bearing off the dial, and move out knowing which way you’re headed. Lensatic does the same for the framework stack: find where you stand, see which framework applies, and take a bearing on what comes next.

I learned land navigation with this compass in the Army and carried it on deployments around the world. GPS did most of the work, but this rode on my gear for the day the tech failed or got jammed. It never needed batteries, and it never lost the signal. More than 20 years later, it still points north. That’s the idea behind this app: when the noise gets loud, go back to the fundamentals that hold.
