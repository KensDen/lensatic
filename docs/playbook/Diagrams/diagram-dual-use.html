Same Horsepower, Two Outcomes

The dual-use reality of agentic AI in the SOC — the same capability, pointed two ways
✅ The best hire your SOC ever made
+
Onboarded in seconds, works every hour of every day, never burns out.
+
Reads every log and correlates across the whole estate at once.
+

Same horn · same horsepower
⚠ The most dangerous insider you ever onboarded
−
Maps your environment as fast as it learns it — the same speed, inverted.
−
Over-permissioned, it becomes an agentic insider with broad reach.
−
Prompt-injected or tool-abused (MITRE ATLAS), it turns on its owner.
−
Exfiltrates at machine speed — walking data out before a human notices.
The only variable is who holds the reins.
Governance is the reins — per-agent identity, least privilege, deterministic containment, human-in-command, and a bounded blast radius. The horsepower is a given; the direction is a choice.
APT_UNICORN is an illustrative framing device, not a tracked threat actor — the point is that defensive and offensive agentic AI are the same technology, and only governance decides which one you have.
