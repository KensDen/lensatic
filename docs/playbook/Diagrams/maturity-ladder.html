SecOps AI Maturity Model

The ladder from manual operations to autonomous, adaptive SecOps
Four levels of increasing autonomy. The human role shifts from doer to reviewer to governor; AI moves from assistant to autonomous operator. You don’t skip rungs: the continuous purple loop is the engine that climbs them.
▲ Autonomy & capability human oversight
1AI-Assisted
Humans do the work; AI gives recommendations and generates content on request.
In practice
  • Claude / ChatGPT for detection rules or IR playbooks
  • Security Copilot for report drafting
  • Natural-language → KQL query generation
  • LLM-assisted phishing-lure analysis
How you recognize itAI is a research assistant; the workflow stays human-driven. Most Tier-1 SOC analysts operate here today.
◆ Most analysts are here today
2AI-Augmented
AI handles defined subtasks autonomously inside human-supervised workflows.
In practice
  • SOAR playbooks calling AI APIs for enrichment
  • AI triage scoring in the SIEM alert queue
  • Automated threat-intel summarization
  • AI-suggested detections in the eng pipeline
How you recognize itAI is embedded in the toolchain; analysts spend less time on low-value tasks; clear human review gates exist.
3Agentic Pipelines
Multi-step agents own end-to-end workflows with human oversight at key decision points.
In practice
  • Triage agent: alert → enrich → verdict → ticket
  • Hunt agent: hypothesis → query → interpret → branch
  • Red agent: recon → exploit chain → report
  • Purple loop: sim → detect → gap → rule-gen
How you recognize itAgents handle full case workflows; humans review, approve, and escalate. All agent runs are logged and auditable.
4Autonomous SecOps
Agents run continuously, self-direct on the threat landscape, and improve controls automatically. Humans set policy and review exceptions.
In practice
  • Continuous purple loop, 24/7 vs. production
  • Self-improving detection: generate → test → promote
  • Hunt focus reallocated from CTI actor activity and AI Incident Database (AIID) trends
  • Autonomous IR for low-risk classes, fully audited
How you recognize itPosture improves passively; coverage trends up without manual sprints; the human role is governance, policy, and edge cases.

This SecOps ladder is the security-specific climb inside that enterprise frame.
