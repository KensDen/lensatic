## The standing carve-out rule

Ken's carve-out from the Dark Constellation naming checks (ruled 22 July 2026) stands for every future name check, Lensatic's included: a released work that has been delisted or unavailable for five years or more, in a different genre or kind, under a title that is not identical, does not disqualify a name. It is noted in the check's record instead. The bar is collisions a reader can actually run into today, so an adjective in someone else's title, a physical object, or a how-to article is a note, not a conflict.

A future check should start from this record and ask only what has changed: a live app, software or security product now named Lensatic, or the near-neighbour turning into a product of that name.
